A simple collection of movies and series I’ve watched over the years, across different languages, cultures, and genres, from romance and coming-of-age stories to science fiction, fantasy, adventure, and drama.

English

The Matrix 1999

A revolutionary film that perfectly blends high-concept science fiction with philosophy and groundbreaking action. It’s the ultimate movie about questioning reality.

Harry Potter Series 2001–2011

A fantasy film series following Harry Potter’s journey through Hogwarts, friendship, magic, and the battle against Voldemort.

Pirates of the Caribbean Series 2003–2017

A fantasy-adventure series filled with pirates, cursed treasures, sea battles, and the unforgettable Captain Jack Sparrow.

The Big Bang Theory 2007–2019

A TV series rather than a movie, following a group of scientists and their friendships, relationships, and wonderfully nerdy lives.

(500) Days of Summer 2009

A romantic comedy-drama exploring love, expectations, memories, and the difference between how we imagine relationships and how they actually unfold.

Inception 2010

Christopher Nolan’s masterpiece on the architecture of dreams. It’s a complex, visually stunning thriller that stays with you long after the credits roll.

Interstellar 2014

An emotional journey through space and time. The scientific ideas combined with a powerful father-daughter story make it one of my favorites.

The Odyssey 2026

Japanese

Love Letter 1995

A Japanese romantic drama (ラブレター / Rabu Retā).

Mandarin Chinese & Japanese

18×2 Beyond Youthful Days 2024

A nostalgic romantic journey about youth, memories, travel, missed opportunities, and a love that continues to live through time.

Telugu

Seethamma Vakitlo Sirimalle Chettu 2013

A warm family drama centered on relationships, brotherhood, family values, and the small emotions that hold a family together.

Yevade Subramanyam 2015

A journey of self-discovery that explores ambition, friendship, happiness, and finding meaning beyond material success.

35 Chinna Katha Kaadu 2024

A heartfelt story revolving around education, family, childhood, and the pressures and expectations surrounding academic success.

8 Vasantalu 2025

A romantic coming-of-age drama about love, emotional growth, memories, and different phases of a young woman’s life.
